What is Blackout Kit?

Blackout Kit is an open-source internet censorship circumvention and DPI bypass toolkit for Windows. It acts as an aggregator for the world's most powerful bypass engines, including GoodbyeDPI, Xray, Psiphon, WireGuard, OpenVPN, Tor, and more. It is designed to bypass Deep Packet Inspection (DPI) in countries with extreme censorship like Iran, China, and Iraq.

Is Blackout Kit safe to use?

Yes. Blackout Kit is fully open-source and operates entirely locally on your Windows PC. Unlike commercial VPNs, it does not collect logs, track usage, or route your traffic through centralized proprietary servers. It includes features like a Mullvad-style strict kill switch, AdGuard tracker blocking, and randomized TLS fingerprints for maximum security.

How to bypass ISP torrent blocks in the EU or UK?

Blackout Kit includes an EU profile that leverages GoodbyeDPI and Cloudflare WARP to effortlessly bypass ISP-level DNS poisoning and light DPI filters used to block sites like The Pirate Bay in the UK and Portugal.
