Zero Logs. Absolute Privacy.

Blackout Kit is designed from the ground up to protect you from tracking and DPI.

No Telemetry, Ever.

Unlike commercial VPNs that route your data through centralized servers they own, Blackout Kit's primary engines manipulate packets locally on your own PC. This means you connect directly to destination websites without any middleman seeing your traffic.

The application itself has zero phone-home mechanisms, zero trackers, and zero analytics.

Three Security Tiers

  • SPEED: Max compatibility. No overhead.
  • PRIVATE: Random TLS fingerprints to avoid traffic analysis.
  • LEGEND: Multi-hop routing (Xray → Tor), encrypted configs tied to your hardware ID, and strict certificate pinning. Near untraceable.

Mullvad-Style Strict Kill Switch & Adblock

If you run blackout shield or blackout killswitch on, Blackout Kit modifies the Windows Firewall at the kernel level. If your proxy drops, your internet is instantly severed, guaranteeing your real IP address never leaks.

Shield mode also sets your system DNS to AdGuard or Mullvad DNS, stripping trackers and ads from your network entirely.
